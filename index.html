Behind the camera

Looking closely. Waiting for the light to say something.

I'm Yashvardhan, a documentary and portrait photographer working across India and beyond. My work sits in the space between planned and found — I go in with a subject in mind and let the actual moment decide the frame.

Most of what I shoot is about people: the ones who let me stand in their doorway for an afternoon, the strangers who nod instead of turning away, the quiet exchange that happens before anyone says a word. I try to leave with more than a photograph — a small piece of how that place actually felt.
